01 / Fundamentals

Blackjack rules at a glance

Blackjack is a comparing-card game in which players compete against the dealer, not one another. The aim is to finish closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Table-specific conditions—including dealer actions, payout ratios, surrender options and number of decks—can change the experience.

Common terms

Hit: request another card.
Stand: keep the current hand.
Double down: increase the wager under table rules and take one additional card.
Split: divide an eligible pair into two hands, usually requiring another wager.
Bust: exceed 21.

02 / Step by step

How to play blackjack

1. Check the table

Review the minimum wager, number of decks, dealer rules, payout terms and any optional side bets. Do not assume every online blackjack table follows identical rules.

2. Receive the opening cards

The player and dealer receive their initial cards according to the table format. A natural blackjack usually means an ace plus a ten-value card on the first two cards.

3. Choose your action

Depending on the total and available options, choose to hit, stand, double or split. Some games also offer surrender or insurance under specific conditions.

4. Compare final hands

After player decisions, the dealer completes the hand under fixed rules. The final outcome and payout depend on both hands and the table's published terms.

03 / Online formats

What makes speed blackjack different?

04 / Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How do you play blackjack?

Try to finish closer to 21 than the dealer without exceeding 21. Review the specific table rules before choosing hit, stand or other available actions.

What are the basic blackjack card values?

Number cards count at face value, face cards count as ten, and aces count as one or eleven.

What is speed blackjack?

Speed blackjack is a faster-paced blackjack table format, often with streamlined decisions and shorter rounds. Exact rules vary by provider.

Can blackjack guarantee a win?

No. Outcomes are uncertain, and no strategy guarantees a profit.

Is online blackjack different from a live-dealer table?

Both can follow similar core rules, but presentation, dealing method, pace and table-specific features may differ.
